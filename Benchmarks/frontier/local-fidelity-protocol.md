Add two local constraints against the **known training reference**: RGB squared error and signed RGB Sobel squared error should not exceed the corresponding cached shipping error in each spatial tile. Average channels within each 8×8 tile, including remainder tiles. Normalize excess by the reference's local energy plus shipping's local error plus 0.0001; detach reference and baseline.
