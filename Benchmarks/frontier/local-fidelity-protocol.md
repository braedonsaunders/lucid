# Local fidelity constraints for the input-conditioned controller

Frozen September 5, 2026 before this experiment. The completed dynamic controller improves development LPIPS/DISTS by 5.70%/6.24% but loses too much true detail. A fresh matched diagnostic proves that the original folded reconstruction passes the same per-source detail limits. This experiment changes the training objective, retaining the 7,408-parameter controller informed by September 2026 SPARK. It is neither a new backbone nor a claim of research novelty.

Add two local constraints against the **known training reference**: RGB squared error and signed RGB Sobel squared error should not exceed the corresponding cached shipping error in each spatial tile. Average channels within each 8×8 tile, including remainder tiles. Normalize excess by the reference's local energy plus shipping's local error plus 0.0001; detach reference and baseline. Average positive excess within each domain. This prevents improvement elsewhere from directly canceling a locally worse tile. Signed gradients penalize displaced structure even if its texture energy is unchanged. The objective does not compute LPIPS, DISTS or the evaluation's Pearson fine correlation.

Use initial nonnegative multipliers [1, 1], an additional quadratic penalty with rho 10, and multiplier ascent `lambda += 0.05 * detached_violation` after each optimizer step. These constants are fixed before training. This is an adaptive penalty experiment, not a proof of constraint satisfaction or generalization. Record both domain violations and multipliers every 200 steps. Compare aligned crops and augmentations explicitly; the baseline comes from the full-context shipping cache, not another differently padded inference.

Everything else remains the completed dynamic controller recipe: frozen area-folded reconstruction, exact channel masks/RMS, seed 20260914, 8,000 steps, batch four, crop 96, AdamW 0.0002 cosine schedule, the existing 50/50 target, reference Sobel/FFT supervision and the released PixRestore DINO adversary at weight 0.005. Run a new unconstrained two-step smoke to reproduce the previous smoke's actual final controller hash, then a constrained smoke with matching initial identities. Evaluate only the final unblended constrained checkpoint against shipping and the completed unconstrained dynamic arm. No new flicker loss, model branch, inference operation, or shipping asset change is introduced.

Use the unchanged 48 full-frame development pairs and 96 bank-validation pairs, with identical scorer/checkpoint/control checks. Require the existing ≥3% LPIPS/DISTS gains, ≤2% per-source perceptual regressions and ≤0.01 fine-correlation losses. If both development screens pass, freeze the model before fresh source-disjoint quality testing and native delivered evaluation. No parameter or penalty sweep follows a failure. Retain all negative results.
