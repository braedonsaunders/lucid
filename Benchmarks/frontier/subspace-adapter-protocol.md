# Mergeable subspace adaptation: fixed matched experiment

September 5, 2026, frozen before training results. Recent unconstrained perceptual training improves appearance distances while losing real detail; freezing an added correction branch, restricting its spatial frequencies, and bounding adversarial magnitude have not produced a joint winner. The 720p folded initialization also fails interpolation quality gates. This experiment changes where reconstruction weights may move, rather than adding another inference branch or choosing a filter coefficient.

The [June 2026 FreqOrtho-SR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.28745v1) motivates separating fidelity and perceptual parameter subspaces. Its actual diffusion architecture is not suitable evidence of live Mac speed: the paper reports 1.30B parameters and 0.30 s in its own benchmark. The [released repository](https://github.com/sonhm3029/FreqOrtho-SR) at `d81826fde0e84646c0c774c3ec6d583669f82210` uses backward projection hooks on semantic LoRA B matrices and AdamW. A projected gradient does not by itself constrain the AdamW weight displacement: coordinate normalization changes its direction. A two-coordinate executable regression test demonstrates this. It does not reproduce or invalidate the paper's empirical image-quality results.

Lucid's experimental parameterization instead fixes an orthonormal coordinate basis and trains coefficients within it. First materialize the shipping 2× area-folded graph's ordinary inference convolutions. For every convolution, compute a CPU float64 SVD of its frozen weight matrix augmented with bias. The protected basis spans the leading singular vectors accounting for 95% squared singular-value energy. The candidate learns only the complementary coordinates; the matched control learns all coordinates in the **same** SVD basis. Both start with zero changes and identical images. Bases and anchors remain immutable. This is an engineering adaptation using the shipping convolution's subspace, not FreqOrtho-SR's pixel-LoRA subspace, mixture of experts or diffusion model.

This preserves each layer's protected linear responses for an unchanged layer input, within FP32 rounding. Changing preceding nonlinear layers can still change those responses and image detail. There is no mathematical guarantee of output fidelity. The constraint survives adaptive optimization because the effective update is constructed from the allowed basis on every forward. Finished coefficients merge back into ordinary convolutions, so no adapters, matrix products, additional pixels or temporal state remain at inference.

Preflight tests must verify zero-update equivalence, a nonzero trainable path, protected effective weight updates after AdamW, merged output equivalence and strict checkpoint round trips. Record graph timing separately from end-to-end performance. The checkpoint type `fused_span2x` is explicit and must not load as the old unfused architecture.

## Preflight outcome

Four subspace/merge tests and four reconstruction-objective tests pass. The fixed nonzero merged graph passes CPU+GPU Core ML correctness at 360p and 720p (worst RGB error 0.999, mean 0.255 levels). Measured inference mean/p95: 5.807/6.167 ms at 360p and 21.341/21.652 ms at 720p, 20 samples after 10 warmups. This excludes native postprocessing, transport and presentation. Both two-step CUDA smoke runs finish successfully with exact BF16 merged-output checks. Their first batch, first output, discriminator, initialization and all three data/cache identities match. The constrained model has 74,924 trainable coefficients; no coefficient tensors remain in its merged checkpoint.

## Completed outcome: reject both candidates

Both arms completed 8,000 steps on the RTX 4080, in 5.263 minutes (full) and 5.298 minutes (protected), excluding startup and evaluation. All seven initial identity checks also match across the full training runs and smoke runs. Every protected checkpoint passes the actual FP32 projected-update tolerance; the largest observed norm is 1.01524e-6. The corresponding unrestricted final norm reaches 0.4261. The constraint works mechanically, but does not preserve final image detail sufficiently.
