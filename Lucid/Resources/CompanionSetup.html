LUCID · BROWSER COMPANION

Your video.
A little more clarity.

Lucid enhances supported, unprotected 144p–480p SDR video on your Mac. Your video stays on your machine.

Chrome or Microsoft Edge

  1. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  2. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select BrowserExtension from the Lucid source folder.
  3. Reload your video tab. Turn Lucid on in the menu bar.

Safari

Build and open Lucid Companion from SafariCompanion, then enable its extension in Safari Settings → Extensions. Allow access on the video website. The Safari companion uses the same authenticated frame protocol.

While you watch

Choose Subtle, Standard, or Strong, and hold See original in the Lucid panel to compare. Turning Lucid off stops frame capture and removes its enhanced surface. Protected video, HDR, and sources outside the supported range remain with the browser.
