Lucid — logo options

Built to match bunkhouse, openbooks and beaconhs: monoline paths that draw themselves, CSS-only with no SMIL, a build sequence that means something, a 12 s redraw cycle, dark mode handled inside the file, and prefers-reduced-motion respected. Resting state is the finished logo, so a renderer that blocks animation still shows it whole.

Each is shown light and dark, at lockup size and at the two sizes macOS renders a Dock icon.

1 · Focus

the concept one

A ghosted second copy of the word sits out of register, then slides in and sharpens until the two become one crisp line — the product performing itself, the way bunkhouse's roof only spans “house”. The mark is a focus reticle that closes onto the lens.

2 · Aperture

strongest small

Five iris blades draw inward from the rim and settle open, then the lens catch-light ignites and the word writes itself — the same build grammar as beaconhs's lighthouse. Reads cleanly at 16 px because it is a ring and a dot at that size.

3 · Resolve

most literal

Four coarse cells draw in, then split into sixteen fine ones that ripple outward — one source pixel becoming the block it covers after a 4× upscale. The most engineering-legible of the three, and the one that needs no explanation to a developer.

The wordmark is monoline paths on the same 100-unit letter grid beaconhs uses, so it is not a font and nothing has to be installed. Accent is one colour per concept — #5bd1ff lens cyan, #f5a623 the amber shared with bunkhouse and beaconhs, or #3ddc84 green. Pick a concept and an accent and I will cut .github/assets/lucid-logo.svg and -dark.svg, the Dock .icns, and the README lockup.
