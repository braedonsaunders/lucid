Lucid — logo options

Built to match bunkhouse, openbooks and beaconhs: monoline paths that draw themselves, CSS-only with no SMIL, a build sequence that means something, a 12 s redraw cycle, dark mode handled inside the file, and prefers-reduced-motion respected. Resting state is the finished logo, so a renderer that blocks animation still shows it whole.

Each is shown light and dark, at lockup size and at the two sizes macOS renders a Dock icon.

1 · Focus

the concept one

A ghosted second copy of the word sits out of register, then slides in and sharpens until the two become one crisp line — the product performing itself, the way bunkhouse’s roof spans only “house”. The mark is a focus reticle: ring, four ticks outside it, lens at the centre.

2 · Aperture

strongest small

Five iris blades draw in as chords of the rim — every endpoint sits exactly on a circle inside the ring, so nothing can poke out — then the lens ignites and the word writes. Same build grammar as beaconhs’s lighthouse, and at 26 px it is still a ring and a dot.

3 · Resolve

most literal

Two coarse cells draw, then eight fine ones pop outward: one source pixel becoming the block it covers after upscaling. The most legible to a developer and the one that needs no explanation.

Fixed since the last pass: the iris blades are now chords of a circle inscribed inside the ring (every endpoint at exactly r=30 against a r=36 ring, so none can escape it); all three marks and the wordmark now share a centre line at y=58, where the marks previously sat 16 units low; and the letter spacing is even — the gaps ran 76/14/24/46 units, and are now 24 throughout, which is the same rhythm beaconhs uses. The wordmark is monoline paths on a 100-unit letter grid, so no font is needed.
